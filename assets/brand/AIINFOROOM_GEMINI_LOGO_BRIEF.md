## 2. What the channel is (and is not)

### Is
- A **tech news explainer** channel covering **AI breakthroughs, inventions, robotics, science with real-world impact, and industry / geopolitical tech shifts**
- **Broader than pure AI** — robotics, deep tech, major model races, product launches, benchmarks, second-order industry effects
- **Evidence-first:** numbers, sources, logos, demos, comparisons
- **Parasocial broadcast energy:** continuous human anchor (Chloe) in a **Virtual News Studio** with floating glass data panels
- Peer DNA hybrid of:
  - **AI Revolution** — cold open claim, kinetic proof, race stakes
  - **Mrwhosetheboss** — human/market cause-chain, friend energy, “who pays / who wins”
  - **MKBHD** — one point of the video; under-claim; evidence before verdict

### Is not
- Celebrity gossip
- Conspiracy / unverified speculation as main product
- Pure trading / finance advice
- Non-tech lifestyle
- Hard-sell affiliate spam or “guru course” energy
- Empty hype / unfounded “this changes everything” without payoff
- A pure coding tutorial channel or pure product unboxing channel (can touch products when they are the *story*)

## 5. Content architecture (what “good” looks like)

### Canonical long-form arc
1. **Hook** (10–25s) — cold open; one concrete claim or tension; **no channel welcome**  
2. **Why it matters** — stakes (money, power, delay, jobs, who can still buy, who wins)  
3. **Explanation** — how it works *once*  
4. **Benchmarks / demos** — evidence that pays off the hook  
5. **Implications** — business / geo / industry / society  
6. **Bigger picture** — race, timeline, historical arc  
7. **CTA** — open-loop question + soft close  

### Storytelling hybrid spine
| Beat | Peer influence | Rule |
|------|----------------|------|
| Cold open | AI Revolution | 0–15s concrete claim; title must be payable |
| Stakes | Mrwhosetheboss | Human or market stakes |
| Point of video | MKBHD | One thesis; real / hype / half-true by the end |
| Proof ladder | All three | Number → source → how it works once → comparison → implication |
| Race / timeline | AI Revolution + industry cause-chain | Who else is in the fight |
| Open-loop CTA | All three | Worth-commenting question; soft CTA |

### Topic fit
**Include:** AI breakthroughs & model releases · robotics & automation · inventions & deep tech · science with real-world impact · tech industry & geopolitical tech shifts  

**Exclude:** celebrity gossip · unverified conspiracy · pure trading advice · non-tech lifestyle  

### Credibility rules
- Cite on screen where claims and numbers appear  
- Prefer primary sources (papers, official blogs, filings, FT/Reuters/Ars-class reporting)  
- Under-claim over hallucinated polish  
- Uncertainty must be flaggable in research
